Privacy policy — Stanza Clock

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This policy applies to the Stanza Clock Chrome extension by Markus Van Kempen. It shows the current time as a word clock on the new tab page. It does not require an account and it does not talk to a developer-operated server.

What we collect

The developer does not collect, sell, or receive your personal data.

The extension does not send telemetry, analytics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers. It does not read your browsing history, cookies, or the content of other websites.

Settings stored on your device

The only permission requested is storage.

Stanza Clock saves your clock preferences locally (and in Chrome Sync if you have sync turned on): letter plate and language; colours, brightness, and seconds effect; new-tab size, timezone, and night-dim schedule; and other display toggles.

Those values never leave your browser except through Google Chrome Sync, which you control in Chrome’s own settings. The developer cannot see them.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No ads
  • No remote code
  • No network requests for time, weather, or updates
  • No host permissions
  • Time comes from the clock already on your computer

Links

The About screen includes optional links to the extension homepage, a portfolio site, GitHub, and LinkedIn. Those open only if you click them.

Children

The extension is a clock. It is not directed at children and it does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
